Creating Child-Friendly Spaces to Improve Social Interaction:
a case study on Wilhelminaoord, a former Benevolent Colony
In this cultural-historically rich village, a spatial disconnect is found due to a lack of public spaces. Using the method of research through design, a Village-Scale and Detailed Design is presented. It is focused on safe and green spaces for children, and the representation of the village’s history through learning landscapes.
